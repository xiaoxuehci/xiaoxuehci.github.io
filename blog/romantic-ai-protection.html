2026 / Participatory enactment and co-design

User-defined Protection in Romantic AI

Study completed · Manuscript under review

How do romantic AI users understand protection, and how could safeguards reflect their own experiences and judgments? This project examines security, safety, and privacy from the perspectives of people who seek safer environments on their own terms.

My contribution

I co-designed the study procedure and enactment activities with another researcher, supported the delivery of two of the four workshops, and collaborated with the research team on data analysis.

Approach

We conducted four participatory workshops with 16 romantic AI users, combining enactment and co-design to explore their experiences and proposals for protection.

Research insights

Participants' accounts showed tensions between protection, consent, and relational continuity. They sought a role in shaping safeguards while also calling for platform accountability and coordination across actors. The project explores how protection can respond to users' judgments and contexts, including meaningful control over data and reachable, accountable platforms.
