2026 / Participatory co-speculation

Knowledge Workers and Workplace GenAI

Study completed · Manuscript under review

What would it mean for workplace generative AI to support knowledge workers when their interests diverge from organizational demands? This project explores workers' own priorities and alternatives to current approaches to AI adoption.

My contribution

As first author, I led the study's conception and research design, developed the workshop materials, and led the literature review, data analysis, and manuscript writing. I also contributed to participant recruitment and co-hosted the workshops with another researcher.

Approach

We conducted three participatory co-speculation workshops with 16 knowledge workers who had experienced organization-led GenAI adoption. Participants developed speculative alternatives and collectively evaluated what these concepts could offer and where their limits might lie.

Research insights

The concepts explored less consuming workplace demands, boundaries on organizational access, and ways to question managerial judgments. Participants also asked whether these forms of support could hold in practice. The project connects workers' priorities with the organizational conditions needed to sustain support, informing discussions of workplace AI design, adoption, and governance.
